Estimate only. Instacart bills FreshPoint on a monthly invoice using one-way driving miles. The customer is not charged this amount at checkout. Actual billed miles may differ slightly from this map.

Formula in production invoicing: base fee + max(miles − 5, 0) × per-mile rate. First 5 miles are inside the base. California warehouses use the higher-cost tier ($38 + $0.90/mi). All other U.S. warehouses on this list use $34 + $0.56/mi. SaaS / platform fees are not included.

Coverage (is this customer inside the 70-minute peak window?) is a separate map. If drive time here is over 70 minutes, treat that as a warning — not a live availability check.
